# Slow Japan Trails

Slow Japan Trails is a small independent shop selling original
print-on-demand goods. Our current collection is funny and sarcastic 11oz
ceramic coffee mugs — every design is our own wording, printed to order and
shipped within the US.

## Shop Pin Publisher

Shop Pin Publisher is our in-house tool that publishes Pins of our own
product listings to our own Pinterest business account, so new designs reach
people browsing gift ideas. It only acts on our own account and does not
read, collect or act on anyone else's Pinterest data.
