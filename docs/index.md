# Slow Japan Trails Shop

Slow Japan Trails publishes practical, slow-paced travel guides to rural
Japan, starting with Shikoku, at <https://slowjapantrails.com/>.

This is the shop side of the same business: original print-on-demand goods
(currently 11oz ceramic coffee mugs with our own designs, printed to order
and shipped within the US) and selected products shipped from Japan, sold
through our eBay listings.

- Example listing: <https://www.ebay.com/itm/318929527170>

## Shop Pin Publisher

Shop Pin Publisher is our in-house tool. It publishes Pins of our own
content — our product listings and our travel guides — to our own Pinterest
business account, so new products and guides reach people browsing gift
ideas and trip plans. It only acts on our own account and does not read,
collect or act on anyone else's Pinterest data.
