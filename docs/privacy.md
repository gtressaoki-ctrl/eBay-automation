# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

This policy covers Shop Pin Publisher (the "app") by Slow Japan Trails,
an automation that creates Pins for our own product listings on our own
Pinterest account.

## What the app accesses

- **Our own Pinterest account only.** The app is authorised by the shop
  owner through Pinterest's OAuth flow and uses the granted access to
  create Pins and boards for our own products and to read the performance
  of those Pins.
- It does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest
  user.

## What is stored

- The OAuth access and refresh tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in the
  automation's private configuration and are used only to call the
  Pinterest API on our own account.
- Product data (titles, images, listing links) is our own content.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

## Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings → Security →
Apps. Revoking it stops the app immediately; no other data is held.
